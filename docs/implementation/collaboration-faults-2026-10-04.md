# Paired specialist fault development check — 2026-10-04

`collaboration_fault_dev_v1.jsonl` defines twelve deterministic test-only injections against the same synthetic two-domain question: order or policy specialist error, incomplete evidence, a conflict once, persistent conflict, forged evidence, or a finding timestamp after its task deadline. `run_collaboration_faults.py` patches one specialist graph at a time around a fresh SQLite/mock-auth HTTP replay, runs `single` and `collab` in randomized order, and writes the usual local manifest, case JSONL, pair JSONL, summary, and HTML.
