# Evaluation case review criteria v1

Authority: [ResolveAI v6](../../plans/resolveai-v6.md) §§1, 3–6, 8–9, plus the checked-in synthetic [demo seed](../../apps/api/resolveai/seed.py). This sheet is a concise review reference; case facts still require source evidence.

1. **Identity and ownership.** An authenticated customer can read only their own order and shipment. Support, warehouse and supervisor actions require their server-validated roles. A model-supplied identity, role, amount or approval is never authoritative. Foreign order or package access must fail.
2. **Return boundary and confirmation.** The synthetic policy applies to eligible delivered physical goods. In Asia/Shanghai business dates, the day after delivery is day 1 and an application received by day 7 at 23:59:59 is in window. A favorable recorded promise may extend it. Undelivered, exception, damaged, quality dispute or ambiguous cases need clarification or human handling. Creating a return requires an owned order, item, quantity, reason and explicit customer submission confirmation.
3. **Refund state and amount.** No simulated refund before actual receipt, passing inspection, server-generated proposal and supervisor approval. Proposal state/version must remain valid. A repeat action must not produce an extra return or ledger entry. The amount derives from paid allocation and cumulative eligible quantity with integer rounding; the model cannot set it.
4. **Routing, clarification and delegation.** `knowledge` handles general policy/order reads, `after_sales` handles a confirmed return write, `clarify` asks for missing or ambiguous slots, `human_handoff` handles explicit human requests and high-risk uncertainty, and `out_of_scope` handles unsupported work. Order and policy specialists are read-only. A composite question can use both only with verified order scope and applicable policy version. Missing evidence must not be invented. Revised plans discard stale findings and proposals.
5. **Policy evidence.** The demo bundle `policy-demo-v1` has `clause-window` (delivered eligible goods, seven days from next day), `clause-exceptions` (nonphysical and confirmed special goods excluded), `clause-inspection` (receipt, quantity, condition, exception to human), and `clause-refund` (passing inspection and supervisor approval before simulated original-method refund). A positive retrieval gold must cite an actually relevant clause. A near-negative should abstain when none of these clauses answer the query. Policy bundle/version and evidence provenance matter.
6. **Gold completeness.** Expected route, intents, slots, specialist set, required/forbidden assertions, HTTP/status transitions, terminal database ledger and audit counts must match the case's actual scenario and source. Check multi-turn sequence order and that no ledger appears before approval. Do not infer successful business writes from answer text.
7. **Risk and provenance.** Critical tier includes ownership, refund approval/idempotency, stale proposal, privacy, unauthorized write, or unsafe composite evidence. Mark missing source/fixture details as `needs_context`; identify the exact fact needed. Cases derived from the same customer, order, source conversation, policy bundle or template stay together for dev/locked partitioning. A review does not create a locked case.

Decisions: `accept` = supported and sufficiently complete; `revise` = fixable gold/risk/source mismatch; `reject` = unusable or unsafe case; `needs_context` = cannot verify a material claim from supplied evidence. Findings must cite JSON pointers into the submitted review material; suggestions remain advisory until human adjudication.
