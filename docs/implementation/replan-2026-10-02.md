# Bounded Replan verification — 2026-10-02

After specialist reads, the coordinator refreshes the owned order and checks its version and policy bundle. General policy questions also recheck the current active bundle. If either fact changed during the read, the answer is discarded and the graph runs again from current database facts with a new plan revision and checkpoint key. The request permits at most two automatic retries and never exceeds revision 4. Continued changes create a support ticket; no partial stale finding is presented as a verified answer.

The isolated tests mutate an order version or activate a new policy during specialist inspection. They verify that the final finding cites the fresh source version and that repeated changes hand off after two retries. The 25-case smoke suite stays at 28/28 passing executions. Authenticated API/MCP checks are rerun after the Docker rebuild.

This covers order-version and active-policy changes in a single request. Evidence conflicts from other sources, reuse of unaffected findings, supervisor amount edits across approval boundaries, and durable graph interrupt/resume still require separate work. Refunds remain under the domain service's approval and idempotency checks.
